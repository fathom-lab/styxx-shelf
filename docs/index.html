what this is / what this is not

  • reproducible, not independent. the lab that writes the ledger also controls this repository, its workflow and its pinned verifier. what a reader gets is a run they can repeat from the printed command and compare digest for digest; not a second party's word.
  • the anchor texts are fetched from the lab's exhibit (anchors/<date>.txt), since the telegram supergroup they were posted to needs an account to read. the shelf checks that those bytes are what the index says, that the proof is over them, and that they name a head in the chain. it cannot see the post.
  • what is outside the lab is the bitcoin block header, fetched from two explorers and compared. a proof attests that the anchor's bytes existed before that block; not that they were published anywhere, not at what url, not that the head covers every send.
  • append-only is checked against the shelf's own record of the previous fetch, kept in this repository. it shows a rewrite between two nights only while that record was not itself rewritten; the record's git history is the lab's too. lines after the last bitcoin-attested head (tonight: 5 of 729) are pinned by nothing outside the lab. a reader who kept last night's report and its .ots can show it existed before the block it names; one who did not must trust this repository's git history.
  • a withheld line is bridged on the lab's word. withheld.json carries a digest the shelf cannot re-derive; the chain is checked through it and the lines it leaves unpinned are counted in the report.
  • a failure is printed, never hidden. exit 1 on any failed check, with the report still written; "pending" is a state, not a pass, and a proof pending past the configured limit is a failed check; a day with no anchor is a count and a list of dates; a verifier that drifted from the pinned copy is said so, and the pinned copy is what ran.
  • what the shelf asks and does not ask: it asks the opentimestamps calendars only to stamp and upgrade its own nightly report (--stamp-self, on in the workflow), never to verify a row's proof: a row's pending proof stays pending here until the row's writer upgrades it. it asks no bitcoin node and no telegram.
